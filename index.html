About the Project

Climate change is one of the major environmental challenges affecting communities around the world. Changes in temperature, rainfall, water availability, air quality and extreme weather conditions can directly affect people's lives.

This project focuses on understanding the impact of changing climate and weather conditions at the local level in Boisar through data collection and community engagement.

Our interactive dashboards and charts provide visual insights into climate patterns, helping residents understand local environmental trends and contribute to solutions.

Major Impact Areas

🌡️ Temperature

Changes in temperature and extreme heat conditions affect agricultural productivity and public health.

🌧️ Rainfall

Irregular rainfall and heavy rainfall events impact water resources and infrastructure.

💧 Water

Water shortage and irregular water supply affect daily activities and agriculture.

🌫️ Air Quality

Changes in pollution, dust and air quality threaten public health.

🌳 Greenery

Changes in trees, plants and green spaces impact biodiversity.

Effects on people's daily activities, health and lifestyle require immediate action.
